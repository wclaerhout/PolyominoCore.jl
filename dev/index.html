Polyominoes.jl

A small Julia package for polyominoes: finite, edge-connected sets of unit squares on the grid. It covers the basics that most work with polyominoes needs:

  • a Polyomino type, normalized for translation, that you can draw and parse as ASCII;
  • rotations, reflections and canonical forms, with symmetry always opt-in;
  • enumeration of fixed, one-sided and free polyominoes, tested against OEIS;
  • splitting a shape into two connected pieces, and joining two shapes;
  • a few properties: perimeter, holes, symmetry group;
  • optional plotting through Makie.

It has no dependencies. Plotting is a package extension that loads when a Makie backend such as CairoMakie is loaded.

using Polyominoes

L = poly"""
#.
##
"""
length(polyominoes(5; symmetry = :full))   # 12 free pentominoes
attachments(L, poly"#"; symmetry = :full)  # the tetrominoes that contain L

Conventions

  • Cells are (r, c) tuples: row, then column. Rows grow upward and columns grow to the right, so (1, 1) is the bottom-left cell of the bounding box. In an ASCII drawing the top line is the highest row.
  • A Polyomino is a fixed polyomino. == and hash compare shapes up to translation only. To identify rotations (one-sided polyominoes) or rotations and reflections (free polyominoes), pass symmetry = :rotation or symmetry = :full to the functions that take it, or compare Polyominoes.canonical forms.

Related packages

Polyomino.jl (singular) generates random polyominoes (Eden model, uniform sampling) and solves chess problems on them: rook and queen placement, guarding sets, minimal line covers. Polyominoes.jl does none of that; it is a general core for the shapes themselves: representation, symmetry, exhaustive enumeration, splitting and joining.
